NonAlcohol Note 한국어

A companion for quitting or cutting down on alcohol

NonAlcohol Note shows how many days you have gone without alcohol and how much you have saved, and helps you log cravings and the drinks you had. It is a web app you can add to your phone's home screen.

Open the app

The app screens are in Korean. An English version of the app itself is not available yet — this page only introduces it.

What you can do

  • Today — alcohol-free days, estimated savings, drinks this week, and a 5-minute “hold on” timer for when a craving hits.
  • Log — record whether you held on or drank (and how many drinks), plus craving strength, mood, situation and what helped; you get one suggestion right away.
  • Stats — drinks over the last 7 days, and which situations and moods come up most.
  • Help — big-text mode and export/import of your own records.

Your records

Records are stored on your own device (browser local storage). Optional research participation, if it is ever offered, needs your explicit consent inside the app. Nothing on this page collects any information.

Please note

NonAlcohol Note is a drinking diary. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for medical care. If you have been drinking heavily for a long time, talk to a doctor before stopping suddenly.
